title: Links
---

- The Morris Worm, propagated on November 2nd, 1988, is often cited as a catalyst for the development of formal incident response processes.
- The Morris Worm exploited vulnerabilities in UNIX systems, specifically targeting a remote code execution vulnerability in the Sendmail process, a buffer overflow vulnerability in the Finger service, and weak passwords of the Remote Shell (RSH) service.
- The Defense Advanced Research Project Agency (DARPA) formed the first Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT) at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) on November 17, 1988. The CERT team developed the first vulnerability disclosure processes, created security advisory systems, and established secure communication channels for discussing sensitive security issues.
- The US Department of Energy (DOE) established its own Computer Incident Advisory Capability (CIAC) in 1989, following CERT's pioneering model but adapting it for more specialized needs. CIAC was formed at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) specifically to protect DOE's critical computing infrastructure.
- Founded by Dr. E. Eugene Schultz Jr., CIAC would fundamentally shape how organizations respond to security incidents. On July 23, 1990, Schultz and his colleagues at LLNL published _Responding to Computer Security Incidents: Guidelines for Incident Handling_, establishing the first formal methodology for incident response.
